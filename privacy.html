Privacy Policy

Information regarding the collection, use, retention, and protection of personal information by IGUER.

1. Introduction

The International Group for Undergraduate Engineering Research (IGUER) respects the privacy of its applicants and members and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how IGUER collects, uses, retains, and disposes of personal information obtained through its website and membership application process.

2. Personal Information We Collect

IGUER may collect the following personal information through its membership application process.

Required Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Year of study
  • University or institution
  • Major or department
  • Publication experience

Optional Information

  • Short Motivation
  • CV / Research Portfolio Link

3. Purpose of Processing

Personal information collected by IGUER may be used for the following purposes:

  • Reviewing membership applications and eligibility
  • Managing membership status and organizational records
  • Assessing research and publication experience
  • Communicating with applicants and members regarding IGUER membership and activities

4. Retention and Disposal

Successful Applicants

Full name, email address, university or institution, major or department, and publication experience are retained while membership remains active.

Year of study is deleted within three months after notification of acceptance.

Optional information, including Short Motivation and CV / Research Portfolio Link, is deleted within three months after notification of the application result.

Upon withdrawal from IGUER, retained personal information is deleted within three months from the date of withdrawal, unless retention is required by applicable law.

Unsuccessful Applicants

Personal information collected through the membership application is deleted within three months after notification of the application result, unless retention is required by applicable law.

5. Form Processing and Formspree

IGUER uses Formspree, a third-party form processing service provided by Formspree, Inc., to receive membership applications submitted through this website.

Information submitted through the application form may be transmitted to and processed by Formspree for the purpose of delivering the submission to IGUER.

According to Formspree's Privacy Policy, information may be processed in the United States and other countries where Formspree operates.

Formspree may also process technical information associated with use of its services, including IP address, browser type, domain names, access times, and referring website addresses, in accordance with its own Privacy Policy.

6. Third-Party Disclosure

IGUER does not sell or rent applicants' or members' personal information.

Personal information may be processed by service providers where necessary for the operation of IGUER's website and membership application process. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or lawful legal process.

7. Protection of Personal Information

IGUER takes reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Access to personal information is limited to individuals who require such access for membership review, administration, or other legitimate organizational purposes.

8. Rights of Applicants and Members

Applicants and members may contact IGUER to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Where processing is based on consent, an individual may also request withdrawal of that consent. Withdrawal of consent may affect IGUER's ability to process a membership application or maintain membership where the relevant information is necessary for those purposes.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

IGUER may update this Privacy Policy when its personal information processing practices or applicable requirements change. The effective date of the current version will be displayed below.
