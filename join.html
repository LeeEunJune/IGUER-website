Membership Structure

IGUER members participate at different levels according to their research experience and academic record.

Senior Member

Members with substantial research and publication experience who may contribute to research leadership, internal review, and mentoring activities.

Researcher Member

Members with established research or publication experience who actively participate in research projects and academic review activities.

Junior Member

Members developing their research experience through collaborative projects, academic interaction, and mentoring within IGUER.

Membership level is determined based on research experience and publication record. Applicants may initially join as Junior Members and later advance to Researcher or Senior Member status.
